About Us

Citrus Tech is a small software company that believes tech shouldn't be synonymous with data harvesting.

Most apps are built around an account and a server. What you type is sent somewhere else, stored by someone else, and used in ways you may never be told about. Sometimes that’s necessary for an app to work - but often it isn’t.

We build apps that don’t need your data. There’s no account to create, and what you enter stays on your phone, not on our servers (or anyone else’s!). Privacy protections are on from the moment you install, and the apps are designed to be easy to pick up, because privacy shouldn’t depend on technical know-how.

How we work

  • Your data stays with you. Our apps don't send what you enter anywhere. It goes into an encrypted database on your own device, and that's it.
  • Paid by you, not by your data. Revenue comes from optional purchases and unlocking extra features, never ads or data sales.
  • Reviewed before launch. Each app's security design is reviewed by an outside security engineer before it ships.
